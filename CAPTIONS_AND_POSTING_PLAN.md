# What The Ground Keeps: 10 carousels + 10 posts

Everything in these designs is a real capture from the game engine (your build, rendered headless) or one of your own screenshots. Every line in quotes or brackets is real in-game text. Nothing is mocked up.

## How to open them in Canva (editable)

1. In Canva, click **Create a design → Import file** (or drag the `.pptx` straight onto the Canva home page).
2. Each `.pptx` becomes its own Canva design at 1080×1350. Every word is a live text box. Every screenshot, gradient and logo is a separate layer you can move, swap or delete.
3. Fonts used: **Bebas Neue** (headlines) and **IBM Plex Mono** (body, the same font as your in-game HUD). Both are in Canva's free font library.
4. Download from Canva as PNG (all pages) to post. Pick **No audio** when Instagram offers a track.

### C04 · Built from code (7 slides)
**Caption:**
This horror game has no artists, no asset store and no music.

Every texture, mesh, character and sound in What The Ground Keeps is generated by code. The shopkeeper's face is sculpted from signed distance fields. The floor tiles and the blood are generated when the game loads. The pump, the bell and the breathing are all synthesized.

Built solo in C++17 and raylib. Follow the build, and wishlist the game if you want to see where it goes.

**Hashtags:** #gamedev #indiedev #proceduralgeneration #horrorgame #whatthegroundkeeps

## Honesty notes (keep these true)

- **All images are real engine renders.** I built your game on Linux and rendered it headless with a free camera, like your F10 editor. For three moments I triggered the game's own event early so it could be captured: the empty "ghost" customer car, the HE LIES stain and the fuel trail fire. These events happen in normal play; I didn't change how they look.
- **Brightness:** some dark scenes use the in-game brightness setting or a light exposure lift. Nothing was added or painted.
- **Guardrails respected:** no music, no women or female imagery (the polaroid, the mother's voice line and the radio tape lines are left out), no profanity (the "what the hell" driver line is excluded), and no spoilers. Zain's deal, the wheel, what the tanks feed and the ending never appear.
- The CTA points to **whatthegroundkeeps.studio**. When the Steam page is live, change the button text in Canva once per design.
